Your Street on the Map

Your street, your voice.

Street by street, we ask residents what it is like to live there. Everything on the map comes from the residents themselves. That way residents, local groups and the municipality can see what works well and what could be better.

Take the survey (in Dutch)Sign up your street

How your street lights up on the map

STEP 1

Sign up your street

You take the lead. We count the houses and give you the link or QR code.

STEP 2

Your neighbours fill in the survey

A few minutes, online or on paper. Only fully completed surveys count.

STEP 3

At 25%, your street opens up

Once a quarter of the houses take part, your street's results appear on the map.

STEP 4

Getting to work together

We bring together what matters to residents and pass it on to those who can act on it.

Would you like your street to take part? Let us know. We'll get in touch to get started together.

Answers are processed anonymously and per street. No names appear on the map, and a street only appears once enough people have taken part.
