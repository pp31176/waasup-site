Join in

Join the WaasUp family.

As a friend of WaasUp, you're the first to hear when an activity goes live. As a volunteer, you help out on the day itself: at the welcome desk, along the route or with setting up. You choose when.

How it works

  1. You sign up here as a friend, as a volunteer or as both.
  2. When an activity goes live, you automatically get a message.
  3. With one click, you let us know if you're joining or coming to help.

Want to share photos of an activity?

Send them to info@waasup.be. We look at every photo first and put the best ones on the site and on our social media.
